Account support

Delete your WalkChamp account

You can permanently delete your WalkChamp account from the app. If you no longer have access to the app, you can request deletion by email instead.

Delete your account in WalkChamp

  1. Open WalkChamp.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Select Delete Account and follow the confirmation steps.

Request deletion without the app

Email admin@miragaming.com from the email address associated with your account. Include your account email or username and use the subject “Account deletion request”. We may ask you to verify account ownership before completing the request.

Email an account deletion request

Before you delete

  • Withdraw any eligible cash balance before requesting deletion.
  • Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Some payment, tax, fraud-prevention, dispute, or other legal records may be retained for the period required by applicable law.
WalkChamp is operated by Mira Gaming. For account-deletion questions, contact admin@miragaming.com.
